Season 16 Magic Gladiator Renewal


1.  Magic Gladiator Build Type

1)   Knight-type Magic Gladiator

(1)   Knight-type Magic Gladiator can be built into either 'Dual-wield One-Handed Sword Magic Gladiator' or 'Two-Handed Sword Magic Gladiator'. 

①  Dual-wield One-Handed Sword Magic Gladiator is affected by Dexterity, and rapidly attacks enemies.

②  Two-Handed Sword Magic Gladiator is affected by Strength, and attacks enemies with powerful attacks.

 

(2)   Knight-type Magic Gladiator new buff skill

①  Spiral Charge

Spiral Charge (Two-Handed Sword buff skill)


Acquired from

Madam Liamann / Strength: 900

Skill Description

Spiral Charge can be used when equipping a Two-Handed Sword.

Once used, it stays in effect until the character dies or logs out.

 

Increases character's ATK and DEF. (Uses strength)

 

Chaos Blade and Fire Blood's range is increased to 6,

and every third skill makes the sword energy to explode, dealing damage to nearby enemies.

 

②  Crusher Charge

Crusher Charge (One-Handed Sword only buff skill)


Acquired from

Madam Liamann / Strength: 900

Skill Description

Crusher Charge can be used when equipping a One-Handed Sword.

Once used, it stays in effect until the character dies or logs out.

 

Increases character's ATK Speed and DEF. (Uses Dexterity)

 

Chaos Blade and Ice Blood's range is increased to 5,

and every third skill causes Sword Energy to explode, dealing damage to nearby enemies.

 


(3)   Knight-type Magic Gladiator new attack skill

①  Chaos Blade

Skill Name

Chaos Blade - Two-Handed Sword & One-Handed Sword skill (MP19 / AG14)

Acquired from

Elveland NPC - Buy from Priest James for 1,000 Ruud

Skill


Skill Description

Strike a wide area in front of and around the character with a blue glow-covered black Sword Energy.

The default range is 3, but using Spiral Charge and Crusher Charge increases the range,

and when the charge buff is activated, every third attack explodes the Sword Energy, dealing damage.

2.  Wizard-Type Magic Gladiator

1)   Wizard-type Magic Gladiator can be built into a Dual-wielding staff Wizard-type Magic Gladiator.

(1)   When dual-wielding a staff, your spells become more powerful.

①  A part of the Wizardry stats from an equipped staff on the left hand (the right side of the slot) is applied to the character.

②  Wizard-type Magic Gladiator is affected by Energy, and attacks enemies with powerful magic attacks.

 

(2)   New Wizard-Type Magic Gladiator buff skill

①  Elemental Charge

Elemental Charge (Staff exclusive buff skill)


Acquired from

Madam Liamann / Energy: 1073

Skill Description

Elemental Charge can be used when equipping a Staff Sword.

Once used, it stays in effect until the character dies or logs out.

 

Increases character's Wizardry and DEF. (Uses Energy)

 

Havok Spear skill's range is increased to 7,

and every third skill causes an exploding Nova that damages nearby enemies.

 


(1)   New Wizard-Type Magic Gladiator attack skill

The default range is 6, but using Elemental Charge increases the range,
