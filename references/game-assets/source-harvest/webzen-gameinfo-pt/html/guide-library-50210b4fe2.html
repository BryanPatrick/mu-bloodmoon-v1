Alchemist

1. Character Info
  1) Character Introduction

  (1) Alchemist is a ‘magic type’ character who uses alchemy using materials.

  (2) Uses Wand (Main Weapon) and Elixir (Secondary Weapon) as weapons.

  (3) She has high defense and magic attack power, and can strengthen magic skillpower using Energy stat.

  (4) Use a party support skill to summon one additional hunting ground monster and increase party's hunting efficiency.



  2) Background Story 

 A girl named Leah, with genius alchemy skills, is born in Elveland, a land inhabited by a mysterious race. She began to shine by learning alchemy 

 when she was eight years old and could read and write, and by the age of nine, she had succeeded in transmuting substances into 'gold'. 

 Her parents and the villagers kept it a secret from her because they knew that she would be in danger if her existence was known to the outside world. 


 However, as the money from selling gold made the villagers' lives better and Leah converting more than half of the materials in the village into gold, 

 this led to her existence being revealed to the outside world. When this fact became known to the outside world, especially to Kundun's ears, 

 Leah's parents were brutally murdered during Kundun's invasion of Elveland in order to take her away.

 With the help of the villagers, Leah was able to escape alone out of the village and escape Kundun, but having experienced everything firsthand, 

 Leah lives with only one animosity towards Kundun, and learns how to fight through her specialty, alchemy, 

 and sets out on an adventure to defeat Kundun at the young age of 16. Carrying the burden of thinking that all the bad things that happened were her fault, 

 she stopped turning things into gold and only studies and uses combat alchemy.
