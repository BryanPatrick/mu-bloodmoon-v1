Distorted Karutan Elite Zone

Distorted Karutan Elite Zone


1. Elite Zone of Distorted Karutan has been opened.
1) Elite Zone
(1) The 'Elite Zone Flag' is placed at the entrance of the Elite Zone hunting ground at the Distorted Karutan.
(2) The Twisted Karutan Elite Zone features the following monsters: Twisted Crypta (Elite), Twisted Gollock (Elite), and Twisted Condra (Elite).
(3) Elite Monsters have an 'Elite Debuff Option' and an 'Elite Skill' and when a character takes damage from an Elite Monster,
they have a 100% of activating ‘Elite Debuff’ and causing damage.

    [Distorted Karutan Elite Zone]

2) Elite Monster
(1) Elite Monsters have the same Immunity as Boss Monsters such as Divine Punishment.
(2) Monsters of each element inflict debuff to character when attacking.
   (3) The effects of these debuffs can be reduced with Guardian Items that have the Elite Resistance Option.

3) Item Drops from Elite Monsters
(1) Aside from the drop items in the corresponding hunting ground, there are items that only Elite Monsters drop.
(2) Some items dropped by Elite Monsters have a weekly maximum drop count
which resets each week.
