  (2) New Silver Heart Weapon Upgrade combination material – Silver Heart Anvil

   ① "Silver Heart Anvil" is a new combination material for upgrading Blue Eye weapon items.

   ② "Silver Heart Anvil" item can be purchased from Elveland NPC James for 100,000 Ruud.


  3) Crafting settings for Silver Heart Weapon

   ① Silver Heart weapons can be upgraded by combining with Blue Eye Weapon with level +11/16 options or higher and the combination material "Silver Heart Anvil".

   ② When upgrading Silver Heart weapons, weapon type, luck options, and excellent options are maintained.
