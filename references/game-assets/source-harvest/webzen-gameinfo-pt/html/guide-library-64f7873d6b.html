5th Class Quest

1. 5th Advancement Background Story


Powerful Monsters with new powers have been found on the continent of MU, which was driven into chaos by Kundun. 

A new force was required to combat the stronger Monsters, and after much sacrifice and research, a new power, “Extra DMG” 

was discovered. Eunice, awaiting the powerful warriors of MU, was deployed to Devias to spread word of the new power.

 

2. 5th Advancement NPC Eunice


Eunice is a Guardian Knight that serves the Benevolent God Lugard, and also the older sister of Cent, one of the 12 Priests of Light. 

Unlike Cent who is skilled with the sword, Eunice is skilled with the giant scythe (war scythe).

 

As one of the Guardian Knights deployed for the discovery of Extra DMG, Eunice is currently on a mission like her sister to 

spread word of the new power to MU’s powerful warriors. Eunice has a friend that specializes in mechanical engineering, 

a profession highly sought after in MU, and can often be seen helping her friend with research.

 

3. How to progress Quest


Eunice (Devias 193, 12)


1) Enter Quest Location (Realm of Privilege, Realm of Trials)


1-1) Realm of Privilege

- The Realm of Privilege is a place to prepare for Quests.

- You can find Party Members to progress Quests together in the Realm of Privilege. 

- The Realm of Privilege can be accessed through “NPC Eunice”, located at Devias [193, 12].

- Only Characters Level 1,200 or above can enter.


1-2) Realm of Trials

- The Realm of Trials can be accessed through “NPC Eunice”, located at the Realm of Privilege.

- If one Party Member enters the Realm of Trials, all Party Members will also be moved to the Realm of Trials.
