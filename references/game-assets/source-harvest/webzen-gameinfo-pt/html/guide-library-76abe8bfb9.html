Alchemist Skills

1. Skill
  1) Uses some skills of the Dark Wizard and some exclusive Transmutation skills.

Shared Skills


Magical Shot, Fire Ball, Power Wave, Lightning, Meteorite, Ice, Poison, Fire Pillar, Tornado,
Hellfire, Telekinesis, Sudden Ice, Death Poison, Hell Burst, Magic Amplification,
 Aqua Beam, Blast, Inferno, Earth Prison, Illusion




Exclusive Alchemist Skill


Transmutation: Angel Homunculus

Transmutation: Ignition Bomber

Transmutation: Countless Weapon

Transmutation: Confusion Stone




(1) [Transmutation: Angel Homunculus] 

Skill Name

Transmutation: Angel Homunculus


Skill Requirements

Energy: 70


Skill Description

Create two Homunculi through the art of transmutation and have them attack,
   damaging multiple targets.



Misc Settings

Cooldown: None


Skill Image



(2) [Transmutation: Ignition Bomber]

Skill Name

Transmutation: Ignition Bomber


Skill Requirements

Energy: 680 / Alchemic Magician



Skill Description

  Creates a stone bomb through the art of transmutation,
  dealing damage to multiple targets in the area.



Misc Settings

Cooldown: None


Skill Image



(3) [Transmutation: Countless Weapon]

Skill Name

Transmutation: Countless Weapon


Skill Requirements

Energy: 1025 / Alchemic Master
Transmutation: Ignition Bomber Skill Required
 Transmutation: 10 Ignition Bomber Points Required



Skill Description

  Use the art of transmutation to create a weapon out of air, 

  launching it to deal damage to multiple targets within range



Misc Settings

Cooldown: None


Skill Image



(3) [Transmutation: Confusion Stone]

Skill Name

Transmutation: Confusion Stone


Skill Requirements

Level: 380 / Energy: 850



Skill Description

Confusion Stones are created through the art of transmutation, and monsters that appear on the field from the Confusion Stones are summoned one at a time for a certain period of time. (Not used in some fields.)

 * The amount of EXP by the summoned monster varies depending on the Transmutation Rate. The Transmutation Rate is affected by the character's Stamina and cannot exceed 100%.







Misc Settings

Duration: 5 min
Cooldown: 5 min

The created Confusion Stone will only be visible to the screens of party members.
Confusion Stones cannot be created more than once per party.
(Even if a party consists of 5 Alchemists, only 1 can be created.)
 Monsters summoned from the Confusion Stone do not drop Zen or items, and will disappear after 1 minute.
