Distorted Karutan

1) Background Story 


Located on the eastern side of the MU Continent, Karutan is surrounded by high mountain ranges, making it a closed and barren region, and is known as one of the regions where travel between regions is impossible.


In particular, the northwest of Karutan was an ashen tower inhabited by various monsters, making it difficult for anyone to approach.Then one day, the nomads of Karutan were able to see strangers from an unknown continent landing, and they cooperated with the strangers to subdue the monsters of the inaccessible Ashen Tower, establishing their own new civilization. 


But it could not last long. In the process of subduing the monsters, Kardamahal's magic circle that had been sealing the powerful monsters underground in Karutan began to crack and reached an uncontrollable limit…


Eventually, with a loud explosion and a loud noise, Kardamahal's magic circle was completely destroyed. When the magic circle of Kardamahal that had been sealing the powerful monsters was destroyed, a large number of the unsealed monsters instantly headed towards the surface of Karutan, causing the mountain range that had been divided based on the central stronghold to distort and form a new terrain. 


As new terrain was formed and powerful monsters from Kardamahal rose through the destroyed magic circle, they took up positions throughout 'Distorted Karutan' and began attacking adventurers.
