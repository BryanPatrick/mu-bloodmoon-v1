Rune Mage Update Story

A. Character Background Setting

  • 1. Summary of Rune Mage Class Story

    The Rune Mage is a half-elf who were born between the mage of Arka, an alliance of nobility, and the fairy who created the Altar of the Wolves.
    As inter-species breeding is frowned upon in the Mu empire, they had to leave the empire and found a settlement called Iria and lived in peace.
    When they came of age, the Lemuria Legion came to the village. They were heading to the front to join the Siege of Crywolf.
    The Arka and the villagers tried to protect their village, but they were quickly overcome by the legionnaires.
    In the chaos, the Rune Mage lost his parents. After the battle, the Rune Mage headed toward Noria for revenge.

  • 4) Rune Mage's Play Type
    • (1) Character Starting Resource Information

      ① The Rune Mage is an offensive spellcaster who amplifies its Spellpower with Energy.

      ② They can cast more than two spells at a time to inflict serious damage over a wide area.


  • 5) Rune Mage Class Quest Setting
    • (1) 1st Class Change
      ① Characters of at least 150 level can receive the 1st class change quest.

      ◈ 1st Class Change Quest
      - Finding The Book of The Warlord
      - The Three Artifacts of Mu (Mage's Spirit)

    • (2) 2nd Class Change
      ① Characters of at least 220 level who completed the 1st class change quest can receive the 2nd class change quest.

      ◈ 2nd Class Change Quest
      - A Rightly Hero

    • (3) 3rd Class Change
      ① Characters of at least 380/400 level who completed the 2nd class change quest can receive the 3rd class change quest.
      ② Upon completing the 3rd class change quest, players can access the Master Skill Tree.

      ◈ 3rd Class Change Quest
      - Level 380: Proving Strength
      - Level 400: Infiltrating the Valgath Barracks
      - Level 400: Into the Darkness

    • (2) Other Items - Pentagram and accessories
