Burning Kethotum Elite Zone

1. Burning Kethotum Elite Zone Unlocked


1) Elite Zone

(1) The “Elite Zone Flag” that represents the Elite Zone is placed on the entrance of the Elite Zone hunting area.

(2) The Monsters Crimson Harpy (Elite), Crimson Minos (Elite), Crimson Lycan (Elite) Spawn in the Burning Kethotum Elite Zone.

(3) Elite Monsters have an ‘Elite Debuff Option’ and an ‘Elite Skill’

    When Players receive damage from Elite Monsters

    they have a 100% of activating ‘Elite Debuff’ and causing damage.

3) Elite Monsters

(1) Elite Monsters have the same Immunity as Boss Monsters such as Divine Punishment.

(2) All attacks of the monsters of each element have a debuff effect.

(3) The effects of these debuffs can be reduced with guardians that have the Elite Resistance Option.

4) Item Drops from Elite Monsters

(1) Aside from the drop items in the corresponding hunting area, there are items that only Elite Monsters drop.

(2) Some items dropped by Elite Monsters have a weekly maximum drop count

and the drop limit is reset on a weekly basis.
