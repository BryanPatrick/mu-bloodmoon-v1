7th Guardian - Tauros

7th Guardian - Tauros

1. Introduction
1) 7th Guardian 'Tauros', 'Tauros (Rare)' have been added.
- Tauros (Rare) is a Guardian with the same stats as the regular Tauros, but with a different color, and can be obtained with a low probability.
- The 7th Guardian is a mount item that is equipped in the inventory.
   - The 7th Guardian can be obtained through a combination of upgrades from the 6th Guardian.

2. 7th Guardian Acquisition

 1) Guardian Upgrade Combination

  - Guardian upgrades can be combined through the NPC 'Trainer' located in Lorencia (122, 110).
  - Upgrading is possible from Guardian +11 level of the previous tier, and the combination success rate varies depending on the level.

  - The required materials for combining items include the 'Seal of Tauros' item and 'Jewel Item'.

  - If the combination fails, the secondary material items will disappear, and the Guardian used as the primary material will revert to level +0. 

  - If the upgrade combination is successful, the Guardian's normal options and elite resistance options will be randomly reassigned.

  - When upgrading a Guardian with 3 general options, at least 2 of those options will be randomly reassigned.  (However, the elite resistance option will always be randomly reassigned.)
