4 Level Seed Sphere

4 Level Seed Sphere


Updates

- 4Lv Seed Sphere (Tetra) has been added.
- 4Lv Seed Sphere can be obtained from Lv. 132 monsters or higher.

Combining

  1. You can combine Seed Spheres via the Seed Master NPC.
    All items are destroyed upon failure.
  2. Combining Seed Sphere (Tetra) requires the following materials.

- Only 1 type of Tetra Crystal can be applied when combining.
- Element of 4Lv Seed Sphere is decided depending on the element of 3Lv Seed Sphere (tree)

What is a Tetra Crystal?

  1. It increases success rate of combining 4Lv Seed Sphere.
  2. There are 3 types of Tetra Crystal and each of them has a different success rate.
  3. 3 Types: Tetra Crystal (Greater), Tetra Crystal (Standard), Tetra Crystal (Minor)
  • Tetra Crystal (Greater): Increases the success rate of combining by 60%.
  • Tetra Crystal (Standard):  Increases the success rate of combining by 45%.
  • Tetra Crystal (Minor): Increases the success rate of combining by 30%.

Unsocket Seed Sphere

  1. Seed sphere dismantle system has been changed to “Unsocket system.”
  2. Socketed seed sphere can be unsocketd After unsocketing seed sphere, you get a seed
    the same element
  3. Seed Researcher can unsocket seed sphere.
  4. When the seed sphere is successfully unsocketed, it goes into the inventory.
  5. When it fails to unsocket, only the material is destroyed while the socketed
    seed sphere remains.
