Season 20-2 3rd Update

Gameplay Improvement

1. Death Penalty EXP Recovery 
1) Addition of Death Penalty EXP Recovery Item
- An item that allows you to recover EXP lost due to Death Penalty was added.
- The item is a paid item sold in the in-game shop.

[Item Info]

Image

EXP Recovery Potion

 lost due to death.

Maximum Number of Stacks

255

   
 2) EXP Recovery Settings 
- Recovery is only possible for regular characters, and Outlaw characters cannot use this feature.
- Recovery is possible within 24 hours from the time you received the death penalty.
- The number of items required for Recovery is determined by the Character's Level and the amount of EXP consumed.
- In MU Red, Recovery is possible for free once a day without consuming any items.
- Up to 10 EXP Recovery entries can be registered, and if the limit is exceeded, the oldest entry will be deleted.

3) How to Recover EXP Recovery
- When a Death Penalty occurs, an ‘EXP Recovery icon’ will appear in the upper left corner of the game screen.
- You can open the 'EXP Recovery UI' through the icon shown.
- You can recover Penalty EXP by clicking on the desired item in the UI window.
       (Warning: If the amount of recovered EXP is greater than the amount of EXP required for the next level up, only the amount of EXP required for the next level up will be recovered.)

3) Changes in Event Map Entry Restriction settings
- The Entry Restrictions for the Chaos Castle, Illusion Temple, and Maze Of Dimensions event maps will be changed.
[Changes in Entry Restriction settings]
- When entering, only the abilities of the Mount and Transformation Ring are applied, and the appearance is not applied.
   - Party status is maintained upon entry.
