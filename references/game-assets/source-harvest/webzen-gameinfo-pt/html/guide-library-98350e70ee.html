Season18 Part2-2 Gore Tarkan Elite Zone

Gore Tarkan Elite Zone


1. Elite Zone

- The 'Elite Zone Flag' is placed at the entrance of the Elite Zone hunting area at the Gore Tarkan.

- In the Gore Tarkan Elite Zone [Gore Bloody Wolf(Elite), Gore Beam Knight(Elite),

  Gore Tantalose(Elite)] are spawned.

- Elite Monsters have an 'Elite Debuff Option' and 'Elite Skill'

  and when players receive damage from Elite Monsters, they have a 100% chance of being inflicted with 'Elite Debuff' which inflicts damage.

3. Elite Monsters

- Elite Monsters have the same Immunity as Boss Monsters such as Divine Punishment.

- All attacks from the monsters of each element inflict debuff effect to character.

- The effects of these debuffs can be reduced with Guardians that have the Elite Monster Resistance Option.

4. Elite Monster Drop Item

- Aside from the drop items to the corresponding hunting area, there are items that only Elite Monsters drop.

- Some items dropped by Elite Monsters have a weekly maximum drop count

  and the drop count is reset on a weekly basis.
