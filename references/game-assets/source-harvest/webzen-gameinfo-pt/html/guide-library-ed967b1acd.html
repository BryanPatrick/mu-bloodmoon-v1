A. New Character - Gun Crusher

1. Gun Crusher 

The MU Empire secretly established the Imperial Corps in order to overcome the attacks of the Kundun forces. They kidnapped a healthy boy from Goa and subjected him to harsh training. 


The boy, who was called Fisher, went through the harsh training of the Imperial Society, eventually losing himself in the training process and becoming a member of the Imperial corps loyal to the MU Empire.


Imperial Society - Appointed as a Gun Crusher, Fisher is ordered to annihilate monsters as his first mission and heads to Elveland.

B. Gun Crusher Exclusive Skills

1. Dark Plasma

Skill name

Dark Plasma

Place of acquisition

Elveland NPC - Marce

Skill

Skill Description

Gun Crusher can fire Dark Plasma at enemy targets or at specific coordinates.
Dark Plasma does not move from the location where it is used,
 and can inflict damage to nearby enemies every 0.5 seconds for 5 seconds.

Main Element

No Element


2. Ice Break

Skill name

Ice Break

Place of acquisition

Elveland NPC - Marce

Skill

Skill Description

When Gun Crusher uses Ice Break in the direction of the enemy,
ice is created in front of him, dealing damage once,
 and then one more time with the broken ice shards.

Main Element

Water Element


3. Death Fire

Skill name

Death Fire

Place of acquisition

Elveland NPC - Marce

Skill

Skill Description

Gun Crusher fires randomly towards the front.
 Attacks constantly with very high attack speed,

dealing damage to enemies at a sectoral range.

Main Element

Fire Element

4. Ice Blast

Skill name

Ice Blast

Place of acquisition

Elveland NPC - James (Ruud Shop)

Skill

Skill Description

Gun Crusher attacks targets around him.
 Ice is created where Gun Crusher fires, dealing damage to the enemy target.

Main Element

Water Element

5. Bursting Flare

Skill name

Bursting Flare

Place of acquisition

Elveland NPC - James (Ruud Shop)

Skill

Skill Description

Gun Crusher makes a strong attack towards the enemy, and at the same time creates a shock wave around the character.
Deals damage to enemies in front, and also deals damage in a range of shock waves.
 If the enemy in front is within the shock wave range, the damage will increase even more.

Main Element

Fire Element
