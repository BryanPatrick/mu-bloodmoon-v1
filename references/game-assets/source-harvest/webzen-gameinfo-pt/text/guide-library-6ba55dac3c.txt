Gold Channel Guide
 Gold Channel Guide
  ▶ A Gold Channel Ticket is valid for a week from the date of your purchase.
  ▶ Gold ticket users can enter to both gold and normal channel.
  ▶ Total 5 Channels (4 Non-pvp, 1PvP)
  ▶ Remaining time on your gold channel ticket will be indicated on the character selection screen.
  ▶ Novice Buff which is originally allowed only for the level lower than 220 is available for all levels.
  ▶ Goblin Point is only accumulated in Gold Channel and there will be a big change for Experience now.
  ▶ for all characters in your account!
