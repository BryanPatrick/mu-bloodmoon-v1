 Kundun Mephis & Lemuria
 1. Character Info
 1) Character Introduction
 (1) White Wizard : Kundun Mephis
  ① Utilizing the opposite Element as that of the Dark Wizard,
      the White Wizard uses Staves and Magic Books as Weapons and holy magic Skills using "Light Energy".
  ② Has low DEF but high Magic ATK.
      You can strengthen your magic with the Energy Stat.
  ③ The "Bond Buff" is activated when in a Party together with Lemuria, his apprentice.
      You can deal more DMG with strengthened magic Skills from the "Bond Buff". 
 (2) Mage : Lemuria
  ① The Mage uses the "Orb"
      to use magic Skills in the form of "spheres". 
  ② Has low HP and DEF but high Magic ATK, 
      and you can strengthen your magic with the Energy Stat.
      Although not as good as the Elf, can use similar support magic that helps in combat. 
  ③ The "Bond Buff" is activated when in a Party together with Kundun, her teacher.
      You can deal more DMG with strengthened magic Skills from the "Bond Buff". 
 * Kundun and Lemuria's Relation
 - Kundun is Lemuria's teacher, and Lemuria is a woman that loves Kundun.
 2) Background Story
 (1) Doppelganger
  ① After the discovery of the "Doppelganger Fortress" in MU Year 1022,
      a Grand Sage from MU infiltrates the Fortress and meets the Doppelganger. 
  ② With the strong intention to stop the evil Kundun, the Sage borrowed the Doppelganger's strength and started researching it, 
      with the research proving successful enough to produce the magic ability to create Doppelgangers.
  ③ A catalyst was needed for a Doppelganger to be created 
      and the Sage needed a Doppelganger of a person strong enough to stop Kundun.
      Objects used by Kundun and Lemuria were obtained with the help of the priests that serve Lugard, 
      and with them as the catalysts, Kundun and Lemuria's Doppelgangers were made.
  ④ The Doppelgangers were identical in not only appearance but also memories, abilities, and personalites.
  ⑤ Under the guidance of their creator, the Doppelgangers developed kind and righteous hearts and the qualities of a White Wizard and a Mage, 
      and when the time came, the Sage sent Kundun and Lemuria on their way. 
      For the purpose of the good Doppelgangers to defeat their evil original selves and save the continent of MU&hellip;.

 2. Kundun and Lemuria's Bond
 1) Bond
 (1) The Bond Buff is applied when in a Party together 
      due to White Wizard: Kundun and Mage:Lemuria's bond with each other.
 (2) The Buff is applied to other Classes in the Party as well.
      However, the Buff applied to them is different.
 (3) The Buff disappears when the Party is disbanded or if Kundun and Lemuria become too far apart.
 (4) The Buff Effect is as follows.

 2) Bonding Cord
 (1) The Bonding Cord is a buff that is applied when in a Party together 
     due to White Wizard: Kundun and Mage:Lemuria's bond with each other.
 (2) The Bonding Cord is only applied between Kundun and Lemuria
     and does not apply between Kundun and Kundun, Lemuria and Lemuria, and Party Members of other Classes.
 (3) The Bonding Cord disappears when the Party disbands, when in a Safe Zone, and when Kundun and Lemuria become too far apart.
 (4) The Bonding Cord can only be seen by Party Members.
