 Summoner Class Renewal 
 1. Summoner Class Build Type 
 1) Chaos Wizard
 (1) The Chaos Wizard with high damage capacity
 The Chaos Wizard uses Wizardry to deal high damage with high attack speed to enemies.
 Through the use of Berserker skills, the Chaos Wizard can increase his Wizardry and Attack Speed.
 (2) Berserker Skill Renewal
 Unlike before, the Berserker skill uses the Summoner&rsquo;s Energy Stat and Curse from Cursed Books to increase the Wizardry and Attack Speed.
 The skill efficiency of the Berserker improves along with the Curse attribute.
 The buff effects of the Berserker is maintained after one use.
 (However, the effects disappear if the character dies or closes the game.)
 Once the Berserker Skills are used, the Cursed Wizards skills are deactivated and cannot be used.
 Curse from Cursed Books: Curse increased by Book of Lagle and such

 2) Cursed Wizard
 (1) The Cursed Wizard, skilled with AOE attacks and debuff skills
 The Cursed Wizard which bases his attacks on Curse, has AOE skills and debuff skills.
 With the new Darkness Skill, he can increase his Curse and Defense.
 (2) New Skill - Darkness Skill
 The Darkness Skill uses the Energy Stat and the Wizardry from Staves to increase Wizardry and Defense.
 When using the Darkness Skill, the Summoner&rsquo;s stats change and the inactive Curse Skill
 becomes active.
 The buff effects of the Darkness is maintained after one use.
 (However, the effects disappear if the character dies or closes the game.)

 2. Summoner Skill Renewal and New Skill
 1) Curse Skill Renewal
 (1) Explosion, Requiem, Pollution Skill Renewal
 The skills Explosion, Requiem, and Pollution skill has been removed from the cursed books
 and can now be acquired through Parchments.
 The skill motion and cooldown has decreased significantly, and the damage that used to be inflicted as DOT has now been changed to normal damage. 
 (2) Weakness, Innervation Skill Renewal
 The Weakness and Innervation skill that used to inflict debuffs on the target has now been changed to an area effect skill.
 When used, a magical field is created around the summoner and the debuff is applied to targets within the affected area.
 Through the master skill tree, &lsquo;decrease target Elemental Attack&rsquo; or &lsquo;decrease target Elemental Defense&rsquo; can be acquired.

 Skill Description
 The Death Scythe Skill uses the Darkness buff to curse a target area.
 When using Death Scythe for the first time, it creates an explosion that deals damage. The scythe follows the summoner and attacks enemies.
 When the skill is not used within 10 seconds, the scythe disappears until it is summoned again.
