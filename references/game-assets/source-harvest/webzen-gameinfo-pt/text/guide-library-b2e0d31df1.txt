 4) Divine Spirit, Divine Horse EXP Acquisition Settings
  1) EXP Acquisition Settings
 Divine Spirit, Divine Horse EXP Acquisition Settings
 EXP Acquisition Requirements
 When a Dark Lord character gains EXP through hunting after equipping the inventory,
 the character gains EXP.
 When you equip 1 Divine Pet
 The equipped Divine Pet in your inventory will gain 10 EXP.
 When you equip 2 Divine Pets
 Each Divine Pet equipped in your inventory will gain 5 EXP.
