 Mysterious Stone
 1.   What is Mysterious Stone
   Upon usages, Mysterious Stone randomly change the current option of (Mastery/Legendary) Set Armors  & Weapons & Shields
    * can be put in storage, exchaged, sold in NPC shop
 2.   How to use Mysterious Stone
   Drag and drop Mysterious Stone on the item you wish to change the option.
    [Caution]
    By using Mysterious Stone, the option may change to the same options as the current options, or to even lower.
   Once gaining the highest option of the each equipment , the Stat Options will remain but only Bonus Options will change.
   * Please note that the range of max option differs in Mastery and Legendary equipment.
