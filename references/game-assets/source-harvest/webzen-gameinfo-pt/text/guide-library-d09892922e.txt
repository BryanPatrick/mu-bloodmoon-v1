 Season 20 Part 1-3 Kardamahal Underground Temple Elite Zone
 1. Elite Zone of Kardamahal Underground Temple has been opened.
    1) Elite Zone
    (1) The “Elite Zone Flag” is placed on the entrance of the Kardamahal Underground Temple   Elite Zone hunting area.
    (2) The monsters ‘Devil Sword (Elite)’, ‘Devil Scythe (Elite)’, and ‘Devil Sorcerer (Elite)’ appear in the Elite Zone.
    (3) Elite Monsters have an 'Elite Debuff Option' and an 'Elite Skill' and when a character takes damage from an Elite Monster, they have a 100% chance of activating ‘Elite Debuff’ and causing damage.

    2) Elite Monsters
    (1) Elite Monsters have the same Immunity as Boss Monsters such as immunity to Divine Punishment.
    (2) Monsters of each element inflict debuff to character when attacking.
    (3) The effects of these debuffs can be reduced with Guardian Items that have the Elite Resistance Option.

  3) Item Drops from Elite Monsters
   (1) Aside from the drop items to the corresponding hunting area, there are items that only Elite Monsters drop.
   (2) Some items dropped by Elite Monsters have a weekly maximum drop count resets after the weekly duration.
