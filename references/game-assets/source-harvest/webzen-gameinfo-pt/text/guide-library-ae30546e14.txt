Protector of Archeron
 Protector of Archeron
 Cursed obelisk will be created in the area of Arca War, one of area of Alkmar, Ubaid and Debenter, every day at 04:00 GST.
 It is not progressed on Wednesday due to Arca War.
 Protector of Archeron detailed process
 1. Beginning Protector of Archeron.
 A message will be notified through the in-game notice that a cursed obelisk has been created. &ldquo;Destroy the cursed obelisk with corrupt spirits.!&rdquo;
 Move to the area of Arca War through Sir Lesnar in Noria and Lorencia. Click the menu, &ldquo;Enter as Protector of Archeron&rdquo;, to enter.
 2. Process
 Hunt cursed monsters and destroy created cursed obelisk.
 Protector of Archeron is progressed for 30 minutes and if you cannot destroy the cursed obelisk, Protector of Archeron will be ended.
 Created obelisk appears with same element of boss monsters as the element of obelisk.
 Boss monsters summon more monsters as time goes by.
 3. Completion Protector of Archeron
 Protector of Archeron is completed, if the obelisk is destroyed with in limited time, 30 minutes, and the boss monsters disappear.
 Waiting time for 1 minute will be given after destroyed the obelisk and you will be moved to Noria.
 When you destroy the obelisk, bundle of jewels or pentagrams will be obtained.
 If the obelisk is not destroyed with in limited time, 30 minutes, the boss monsters disappear. Waiting time for 1 minute will be given and you will be moved to Noria.
