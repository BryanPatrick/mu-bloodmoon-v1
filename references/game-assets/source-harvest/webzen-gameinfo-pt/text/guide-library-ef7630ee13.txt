 Swamp of Destruction Elite Zone
 1. The elite zone of the Swamp of Destruction has been opened.
  1) Elite Zone
- The Elite Zone of the Swamp of Destruction has been opened, and an 'Elite Zone Flag' symbolizing the elite zone has been erected at the entrance.
   - Here, Elite Monsters that use ‘Elite Debuff options’ and ‘Elite Skills’ appear. When a character is attacked, there is a 100% chance that 'Elite Debuff' will be applied, causing them to take damage.

  2) Elite Monster
- Elite Monsters possess the same Immunity treatments as boss monsters, including immunity to Punish.
   - The 'Elite Debuff' applied based on element is applied to the character through all of the monster's attacks, and the character can reduce the debuff effect with the Guardian's elite resistance option.

  3) Elite Monster Drop Item
- In addition to the common items dropped in hunting grounds, there are also items that can only be obtained from Elite Monsters.
   - This item has a maximum drop limit, and the drop limit is reset every week.
 Drop Item
 Item Explanation
 Sealed Armor Fragment
 You can craft one piece of 'Cracked Bloodangel Sealed Armor'
 using 50 Sealed Armor Fragment items.
 When using Cracked Bloodangel Sealed Armor, you can craft a Random Class +13 Level Bloodangel
 Armor based on the success rate without consuming a Bloodangel Soul.
 Elite Guardian Upgrade Stone
 This is the Upgrade Stone required to upgrade Guardian items of level +11 or higher.
 Elite Option Conversion Fragment
 When you obtain 50 Elite Option Conversion Fragments,
 an Elite Option Conversion Stone will be created, which allows you to change the Elite options of your Guardian.
 Golden Crest
 An item used as a crafting material for Lv. 4 Wing (Cape).
 Garuda's Flame
 An item used as a crafting material for Lv. 4 Wing (Cape).
 Uriel's Feather
 An item used as a crafting material for Lv. 5 Wing (Cape) Divine Object.
 Jewel of Harmony
 A jewel that allows you to add specific options to items of level +9 or higher.

   - The Elite Zone in the Kardamahall Underground Temple will be removed, and regular monsters will appear in the hunting ground.
