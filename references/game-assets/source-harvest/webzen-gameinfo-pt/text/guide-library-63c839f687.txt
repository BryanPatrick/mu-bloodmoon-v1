 Rune Mage Update Story
 A. Character Background Setting
 1. Summary of Rune Mage Class Story The Rune Mage is a half-elf who were born between the mage of Arka, an alliance of nobility, and the fairy who created the Altar of the Wolves.
As inter-species breeding is frowned upon in the Mu empire, they had to leave the empire and found a settlement called Iria and lived in peace.
When they came of age, the Lemuria Legion came to the village. They were heading to the front to join the Siege of Crywolf.
The Arka and the villagers tried to protect their village, but they were quickly overcome by the legionnaires.
In the chaos, the Rune Mage lost his parents. After the battle, the Rune Mage headed toward Noria for revenge.

 (1) Magic Arrow Skill name Magic Arrow
 Pentagram
main element Darkness
 Requirement 142 Energy
 Description Magic Arrow can only be learned from the skill tome.
It is a powerful early spell for the Rune Mage.
Upon casting, the caster creates three magical arrows and shoots them to the enemies.
Each magic arrow explodes upon contact, inflicting damage to the target and two additional enemies within 2 tiles.

 Pentagram
main element Darkness
 Requirement 2nd class change/600 Energy
 Description Plasma Ball is a magic skill that can be learned
from a skill tome after the 2nd class change.
Upon casting, a ball of rune energy slowly travels 4 tiles
and inflicts damage to enemies within 5 tiles.
This skill is more useful when it is used as a triggered spell (delay: 0 sec) in combination with other offensive spells than when it is used alone.

 Pentagram
main element Darkness
 Requirement 3rd class change/Plasma Ball Mastery 10P/ 1180 Energy
 Description Lightning Storm is a magic skill that can be learned
from a skill tome that James sells at the Ruud Shop.
Upon casting, the rune energy ascends to the skies.
When the energy descends and hits the ground, it explodes, inflicting severe damage to up to eight enemies within 6 tiles from the epicenter.
