 1. Gore Tarkan
 1) Background Story
 Tarkan, a place failed experiment subjects from Kanturu have gathered and live.
 Deep within Tarkan, a place covered in sand as red as blood was discovered.
 With the discovery of strangely red sand,
 scientists from MU Continent gathered to analyze the sand.
 However, no one could come up with an explanation to why the sand has turned red.
 It was just said, 'Sand is under some sort of spell.'Which was also just a speculation.
 With little to no discoveries made, further studies were required.
 Yet, now more aggressive failed experiment subjects from Kanturu are roaming around and hindering even just approaching the place.
 Hence, they have asked the warriors in MU Continent to handle the failed experiment subjects.
