Cash Shop/Inventory
 Cash Shop/Inventory

 [In-game Shop preview]
  
  
 [Opening In-game Shop]
 - To open in-game Shop, you can either press hotkey X, or click &lsquo;In-game Shop&rsquo; button at the left bottom side.
 - In-game shop is consist of three sections; 1) Category, 2) Item List, and 3) Vault.
 1) Category: this shows the list of item category.
 2) Item List: if you choose a category, you can see the list of items. You can purchase items by clicking &lsquo;Buy&rsquo; button. Please check the period of item carefully before you purchase items.
 3) Vault: once you purchase an item, it will automatically go to your storage(vault). If you want to use it, click the item and then &lsquo;Use&rsquo; button. It will move to your inventory.
 [Using Web-Shop]
 - To purchase items via web-Shop, go to Shop menu and select &lsquo;Item Shop&rsquo;.
 - To purchase items, you should login with your account.
