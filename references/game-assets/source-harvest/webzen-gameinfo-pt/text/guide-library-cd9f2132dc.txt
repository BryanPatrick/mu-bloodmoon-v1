UI Change
 UI Change
  
  
 MU Online UI has been changed to enhance player experience in game playing
  
 Search Private Store
  
 1) Players are now be able to search items in private store in any town in safezone (Using keyboard 'P' or under the menu in 'U')
  
  
  
 2) Players are now be able to list their selling items not just in amount of ZEN but also with number of jewels
  
  
 3) Also, there is a special board called 'Private Store Bulletin Board' in Loren Market (138,142) & (110,134) where players can look up the private stores as well
  
  
  
  
 Map appearance control
  
 1) Players are now be adjust transparency of map using keyboard '[' and ']'
  
 2) Also the size of map will be controllable with '+' and '-' key
