 1. Earring of Frost
  1) The 8th Mastery earring, 'Earring of Frost' has been added.
- Mastery Earrings provide a 'Earring Set Option' effect when you wear both the left and right earrings of the same tier.
   - You can enhance items up to level 15 using the Lapidary Stones obtained through the Chaos Goblin NPC.

  2) Earring of Frost Option
- Earring of Frost can generate 1 to 3 options

  3) Earring of Frost Acquisition
- Earring of Frost can be acquired through Upgrade Combination of 7th Mastery Earring, 'Earring of Foliage'.
- Earring of Frost can be crafted by combining Earring of Foliage of Lv.11~15 with the 'Casting Mold of Frost' crafting material.
- Once upgraded, the options of the earrings will be imbued with 1-3 options at random.
   - Casting Mold of Frost can be bought for 25,000 Ruud via Priest James
