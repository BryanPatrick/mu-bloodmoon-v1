 Kanturu Underground Elite Zone
 1. The Elite Zone of Kanturu Underground has been opened.
   1) Elite Zone
     (1) The ‘Elite Zone Flag’ is placed on the entrance of the Elite Zone hunting area at the Kanturu Underground map.
     (2) Elite Monsters Spawn in Kanturu Underground Elite Zone
     (3) Elite Monsters have an ‘Elite Debuff Option’ and an ‘Elite Skill’   and when players receive damage from Elite Monsters, they have a 100% of being inflicted by ‘Elite Debuff’ which deals damage.

   2) Elite Monsters
     (1) Elite Monsters have the same Immunity as Boss Monsters such as Divine Punishment.
     (2) All attacks of the monsters of each element have a debuff effect.
     (3) The effects of these debuffs can be reduced with guardians that have the Elite Resistance Option.

   3) Item Drops from Elite Monsters
     (1) Aside from the drop items in the corresponding hunting area, there are items that only Elite Monsters drop.
     (2) Some items dropped by Elite Monsters have a weekly maximum drop count   which resets each week.
