 1. New Hunting Ground: Kardamahal Underground Temple
 2. Background Story:
 Etramu Renos, the great wizard of the ancient kingdom of Arca, sacrifices his life to seal Kundun.
 The emperor of the MU Empire, Muren Rexmillon, who united Lorencia and Arca, built the 'Kardamahal Underground Temple' in the southern underground cave of Karutan using the relics left behind by Etramu to mark his achievement.
 Kundun learns that the relics of the Kardamahal Underground Temple contain Extra power, and he summons demons to take over the temple in order to gain that power.
 The noble temple is destroyed by the summoned demons, and even the flowing water turned red.
 Upon learning of this, the heroes of the MU Continent join the battle to reclaim the ‘Kardamahal Underground Temple’.
