 Tormenta Island Elite Zone
 1) Elite Zones
 The 'Elite Zone Flag' is placed at the entrance of the Elite Zone hunting area at the Tormenta Island.
 The Monsters Storm Guard (Elite) / Storm Reaper (Elite) / Storm Fiend (Elite) appear in the Tormenta Island Elite Zones.
 Elite Monsters have an 'Elite Debuff Option' and 'Elite Skill', and when players receive DMG from Elite Monsters,
 they have a 100% of activating ‘Elite Debuff’ and causing DMG.

 3) Elite Monsters
 Elite Monsters have the same Immunity as Boss Monsters such as Divine Punishment.
 All attacks of the monsters of each element have a debuff effect.
 The effects of these debuffs can be reduced with Guardian Items that have the Elite Resistance Option.

 4) Item Drops from Elite Monsters
 Aside from the drop items in the corresponding hunting area, there are items that only Elite Monsters drop.
 Some items dropped by Elite Monsters have a weekly maximum drop count which resets each week.
 Dropped Item

 Elite Option Conversion Fragment
 When 50 Elite Option Conversion Fragments are collected,
 Elite Option Conversion Stones that allow the user to change Guardian Elite Options are created.
 Sealed Armor Fragment
 When you collect 50 Sealed Armor Fragments, you can craft 1 Cracked Bloodangel Armor parts.
 The Cracked Bloodangel Armor has a chance to craft Bloodangel without a Bloodangel Soul, depending on chance, and if the crafting is successful, a +13 Bloodangel Armor can be received.
