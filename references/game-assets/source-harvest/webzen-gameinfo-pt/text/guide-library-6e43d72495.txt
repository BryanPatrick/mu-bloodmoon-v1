 A. Dark Knight Class Renewal
 1. Fury Knight
    - Increases hunting efficiency by increasing the attack skill range
      and the damage range of Blow type skills using the Sword's Fury skill.
  1) New Skill
   (1) Sword's Fury
 Sword's Fury 
 Required Stats
 Strength : 1060
 The Dark Knight's Sword Fury skill is maintained once it is used.
 After use, the range of the attack skill and the range of the blow type skills increases.
   (2) Sword Blow
 Sword Blow 
 Required Stats
 Strength : 1090
 A Water Type Blow skill that causes damage over a wide range.
 Unlike Fire Blow, it deals damage by attacking in a wide damage range.
 2. Belief Knight
    - It has the effect of changing stats and increasing Base DEF through Firm Belief skill.
    - After changing into a Belief Knight, you can use Strong Blessing skill and increase the ATK and survivability of party members.
  1) New Skill
 Firm Belief
 Required Stats
 Energy : 1040
 The Dark Knight&rsquo;s Firm Belief skill is maintained once it is used.
 It requires equipping a shield, and when used, changes into Belief Knight,
 increasing the efficiency of Defense (General/Elemental) through stats and increasing the Base DEF.
 Strong Blessing
 Required Stats
 Energy : 1052
 A skill that can be user after changing into Belief Knight.
 A certain amount of party member's Max HP is converted to HP and absorbed using Dexterity Stats.
 In addition, it is a skill that receives a certain amount of party members&rsquo; (General/Elemental) Damage 
 and increases their attack by using Energy Stats.

 3.  Other Options and Changes 
  1) New Mastery Shield Option
   (1) DMG taken instead decreases by amount equal to Shield DEF *0.1   
    ① Option value increases according to the item&rsquo;s Upgrade Level through Shield options created in Mastery Shield.
    ② When receiving a part of the damage of a party member through the Strong Blessing skill,
        an option reduces the damage received instead to within the maximum value.
    ③ This option uses the Shield&rsquo;s defense to reduce damage, and so its effects increase when Shield&rsquo;s defense in high.
  2) Changes in Required Stats settings
    ① The available stats of the Mastery Sword are changed to Strength Stats or Energy Stats.
    ② You can equip it even if you are satisfied with only one of the two Strength or Energy Stats.
