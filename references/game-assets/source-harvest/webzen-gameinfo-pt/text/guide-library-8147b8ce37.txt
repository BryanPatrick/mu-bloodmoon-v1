 How To Get Skill Books?
 You can get skill books from either NPC or Monsters
 NPC can be found by coordinate
 How To Use Skill Books?
 1. Purchase skill books NPC
 2. Click mouse right button on skill books to learn skills.
 3. Click the 1 , 2 by mouse left button to select a skill to use.
 4. Click mouse right button over monsters. Then, skill will be activated.
 How To Trade With Users?
