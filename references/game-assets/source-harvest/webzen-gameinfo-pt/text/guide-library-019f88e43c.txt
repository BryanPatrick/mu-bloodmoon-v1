MU Lite
 MU Lite
 1. What is MU Lite?
 MU Lite is an event application which can be enjoyed by linking PC MU Online game account and is open for all global users.
 By playing MU Online, you may acquire Dimensional Key and receive MU Lite items by using them.
 On the other hand, when you play MU Lite, you may acquire Hero Token which is used for the MU Online items as a rewards.
 2. What is the minimum system requirement?
 Minimum Requirements: Android 6.0 / iOS 12.0
 If you use a version of OS lower than the minimum requirement, it may not be smooth to use the game,
 and it may not be possible to support specification-related inquiries and troubleshooting through customer support.
 3. What is new in MU Lite Season 2?
 Unlike the previous Season of MU Lite, the game has been renewed with more dynamic and strategic elements including view
 switch (landscape to portrait switch), moving direction change, and new systems of conquering towers.
 There are three modes of matches: Season Mode, League Mode, and Single Mission Mode.
 Season Mode is the main mode for the new MU Lite. Egg and Points can be obtained through victory at Season Mode. The Season Mode can be enjoyed by 1:1 or 2:2. Rewards can vary depending on the points.
 League Mode is open 3 times a day and can be participated only when the League Mode is open. Through victory, you can reach higher Tier and Ranking. Loss will bring points deduction.
 Single Mission Mode can be played by using an energy ticket and will allow you to face the NPC. Upon victory, unit cards, gold and other rewards can be acquired.
 4. How do I acquire Gems?
 Gems can be purchased through MU Online web-shop. After purchase, the purchased gem will be shown at game lobby.
 * Please tap the refresh button next to the gem to apply the purchased gem.
 5. How can I get an Energy Ticket?
 Energy tickets will be recharged after a certain amount of time. It can also be purchased by using gem or obtained by watching advertisement.
 6. What is Hero Token?
 Hero Token can be used to spin the Hero Token Roulette. Roulette offers various PC MU online items, and the Hero Token can be acquired through game play of MU Lite.
 7. What is Dimensional Roulette?
 Dimension Key can be acquired by hunting monsters at PC MU Online. The number of Dimension key in possession can be viewed at MU Online banner.
 The Dimension Key can be used to spin the Dimension Key Roulette, and you can acquire transformation stone.
