 How to purchase items
 Here are the steps for purchasing items for your character from the online store.
 Buying items is not difficult! Just follow these steps:
 Step 1.
 Go to MU Online's online item shop. There are various items that can be purchased with Wcoin.
 Click ‘here’ to go to the ‘item shop’ page.
 Click ‘here’ to go to the ‘top-up’ guide page.
 Step 2.
 Select a category to find the item you want to buy and click on ‘Buy Now’.
 Step 3.
 Confirm your order and click on 'Buy' to complete the order. You can check your remaining Wcoin balance on the Purchase Checkout page.
 note:
 - Purchased items are valid for 1 year from the date of purchase.
 - Unused items will be permanently deleted 1 year after the date of purchase.
 - Some items can only be [used] in the game.
 - Items marked with [SPEED] can only be used in SPEED SERVER.
 Step 4.
 Click 'Inbox' to view the purchased items or click 'OK' to stay on the store page to purchase additional desired items.
 Click ‘here’ to go to the ‘Inbox’ page .
 Step 5.
 There are 3 categories on the Shopping List page where you can check your items and their status. You can delete items where if deleted the item will be permanently deleted.
 Step 6.
 Log in to your character and press 'X' to open the in-game X-shop. Select an item and click 'Use' to move the item to your character's storage.
