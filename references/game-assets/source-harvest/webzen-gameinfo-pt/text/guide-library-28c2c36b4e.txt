 How to Top-Up
 Here are the steps to add money to your MU account.
 Adding money to MU is not difficult! Just follow these steps:
 Step 1.
 Go to the MU Online website and select your preferred login method. Log in with your WEBZEN, Facebook or Google account.
 Click ‘here’ to go to the Top Up page.
 Note: These 3 login methods are different. The same account is required to log in and top up.
 Step 2.
 Click on MU and click "Continue".
 Step 3.
 Choose a payment method. and desired package After that, click on “Continue” (click ‘More payment methods’ to see other payment methods)
 Note 1: Payment currency including Bonus Wcoin may vary per payment channel and package. Please check the currency and Bonus Wcoin before making payment.
 Note 2: During a Bonus Cash event, the event end time is displayed on the top
 Step 4.
 Confirm your order and click 'Continue' to pay.
 Step 5.
 Select payment channel and click ‘Make Payment’.
 Step 6.
 Scan the QR with your mobile banking app.
 Step 7.
 If payment is successful, you will be able to check your balance amount on the 'Transaction History' page.
 Click here to go to the Transaction History page.
